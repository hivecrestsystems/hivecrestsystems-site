**The short answer:** a small Shopify store costs $29–$39 a month for the plan and about 3% of every online sale in card fees ([Shopify][shopify]). Setup runs $500–$3,000 if you do it yourself, $2,000–$10,000 with a freelancer, and $8,000 or more with an agency, and a freelancer can launch a small store in 2–4 weeks ([Ecosire][ecosire]).

The plan is rarely the biggest part of the bill. What you pay depends mostly on who builds the store and how much you sell, because card fees grow with every order while the plan stays flat. This guide covers Shopify's own fees, payment processing, themes and apps, setup costs, three worked first-year budgets, timelines, and when Shopify is the wrong choice, so you can budget for the whole year rather than the monthly plan alone.

## At a glance

| Who builds it | Setup | Monthly fixed costs | Time to launch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You | $500–$3,000 in the first year, including a $0–$500 theme | $29–$39 plan, plus $20–$50 in apps | 3–6 weeks |
| Freelancer | $2,000–$10,000 | $29–$39 plan, plus $50–$120 in apps | 2–4 weeks |
| Small agency | $8,000–$25,000 | $79–$105 plan, plus $100–$500 in apps | 6–12 weeks |
| Established agency or Shopify Plus partner | $25,000–$50,000+ | From $2,300 for Shopify Plus, plus apps | 3–4 months |

Card fees come on top of every route: 2.9% + 30¢ per online order on the Basic plan, falling to 2.25% + 30¢ on Plus, when you use Shopify Payments, Shopify's own card processor ([Shopify][shopify]). Setup ranges and timelines are from [Ecosire][ecosire] and [Elementor][elementor]. The app budgets come from the surveys in [themes and apps](#themes-and-apps).

**In Maryland, add 3% to the setup bill.** Since July 1, 2025, Maryland charges 3% sales tax on custom web design and custom programming ([Comptroller of Maryland][md-tax]), so a $5,000 build carries $150 in tax. The [Maryland section](#if-your-business-is-in-maryland) covers the rest.

## Shopify's own fees

Shopify's four main plans run from $29 to $2,300 or more a month, and paying for a year up front cuts the price by about 25% ([Shopify][shopify]).

| Plan | Billed monthly | Billed yearly, per month | Extra staff accounts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $39 | $29 | None |
| Grow | $105 | $79 | 5 |
| Advanced | $399 | $299 | 15 |
| Plus | From $2,300 | Not offered | Unlimited |

Prices are in US dollars, from Shopify's pricing page ([Shopify][shopify]). A few other fees and offers sit around the plan:

- **Trial:** 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months ([Shopify][shopify]).
- **Selling in person:** POS Pro, Shopify's point-of-sale upgrade for physical shops, costs $89 a month per location ([Shopify][shopify]).
- **Sales tax calculation:** Shopify Tax is free until your store reaches $100,000 in lifetime sales. After that it charges 0.35% per order on Basic, Grow and Advanced, capped at 99¢ an order, in the states where you collect tax. Stores created on or after May 13, 2026 have no yearly cap on these fees ([Shopify Help][shopify-tax]).
- **Domain and email:** neither comes with the plan. A domain costs $10–$25 a year, and business email $6–$12 per person a month ([Ecosire][ecosire]).

### When a higher plan pays for itself

The higher plans charge less per card sale, so at some sales level the lower rate covers the higher price. Working from Shopify's published rates ([Shopify][shopify]):

- **Basic to Grow:** Grow's online card rate is 0.2 percentage points lower and costs $66 more a month. It pays for itself at about $33,000 a month in card sales, or $25,000 if you pay yearly.
- **Grow to Advanced:** Advanced costs $294 more a month for another 0.2 points off. It breaks even at about $147,000 a month in card sales, or $110,000 on yearly billing.

Below those levels, the only reasons to upgrade are the extra staff accounts and reports.

## What card processing costs

Card processing takes about 3% of every online sale, and it's the one Shopify cost that rises with your revenue. A store on Basic selling $10,000 a month, at an average order of $60, pays about $340 a month in card fees, nearly nine times its $39 plan (arithmetic from [Shopify's rates][shopify]). Past about $10,000 a month in sales, processing usually costs more than everything else combined ([Ecosire][ecosire]).

| Plan | Online card rate | In-person card rate | Fee for using another processor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | 2.9% + 30¢ | 2.6% + 10¢ | 2% |
| Grow | 2.7% + 30¢ | 2.5% + 10¢ | 1% |
| Advanced | 2.5% + 30¢ | 2.4% + 10¢ | 0.6% |
| Plus | 2.25% + 30¢ | 2.3% + 10¢ | 0.2% |

The card rates apply with Shopify Payments. The last column is what Shopify charges if you use a different card processor, on top of that processor's own fees ([Shopify][shopify]).

### Other payment fees

| Fee | What it costs |
| --- | --- |
| Charging shoppers in their own currency | A 1.5% currency conversion fee for US stores ([Shopify Help][shopify-markets]) |
| International cards | About 1% extra ([eSEOspace][eseospace-fees], citing Shopify's Help Center) |
| Running another processor beside Shopify Payments | A 1.25% premium on your Shopify Payments transactions ([eSEOspace][eseospace-fees], citing Shopify's Help Center) |
| A chargeback you lose (a customer disputes the charge with their bank) | The order amount plus a $15 fee ([Chargebacks911][chargebacks911]) |
| Refunds | Card fees and currency conversion fees aren't returned ([Shopify Help][shopify-markets]) |
| PayPal, for comparison | 2.99% + 49¢ for cards; 3.49% + 49¢ for PayPal Checkout; $20 per chargeback ([Shopify blog][shopify-blog]) |

- **Shopify Payments has no setup or monthly fee** beyond the card rate and the plan ([eSEOspace][eseospace-fees]).
- **The fee for another processor is the main trap.** A Basic store that runs $150,000 a year through a different card processor pays Shopify an extra $3,000 at 2%, plus that processor's own rate ([Shopify][shopify]). Unless you have a specific reason not to, Shopify Payments is the cheaper choice.
- **Check eligibility if you sell restricted products.** Shopify Payments isn't open to every type of business. If yours is excluded, you'd pay the other-processor fee on every sale, so confirm your eligibility with Shopify before you budget.

## Themes and apps

A theme is a one-time cost of $0–$500 ([ShopThemeDetector][std-themes]). Apps are a monthly cost that most stores keep under $100 ([Eightx][eightx]), although agencies often budget several hundred ([Ecosire][ecosire]).

### Themes

| Option | Cost | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Shopify theme | $0 | The Theme Store had 24 free themes on November 3, 2026 ([Vincent Schmalbach][schmalbach]) |
| Paid Theme Store theme | $100–$500 once; median $315 ([ShopThemeDetector][std-themes]) | Half of paid themes cost $220–$360 ([ShopThemeDetector][std-themes]). One license covers one store, and sales are final ([eSEOspace][eseospace-themes]) |
| Customizing a theme | $1,000–$8,000 ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | Clean Commit gives $1,000–$5,000 ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) |
| Fully custom theme | $10,000–$40,000+ ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | Clean Commit gives $5,000–$30,000, and $20,000–$50,000 at premium agencies ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) |

You can try a paid theme in your store before you buy it. Its updates and support come from the theme's developer, not from Shopify ([Vincent Schmalbach][schmalbach]).

### Apps

Published figures on app spending disagree, because they measure different stores:

| What was measured | Monthly app spend | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All 3.59 million tracked stores | 98% spend under $100; only 1.8% spend more | [Eightx][eightx], using Store Leads data from May 2026 |
| 300 busy stores | Median $51.99 across 11 apps; the top tenth spend $361 or more | [Koala Apps][koala], 2026 |
| Store owners on average | About $120; plan for $100–$150 once the store is fully running | [ShopThemeDetector][std-apps], 2026 |
| Established stores, agency estimate | $100–$500 | [Ecosire][ecosire] |

- **Starting budget:** $20–$50 a month is realistic at launch. Expect it to rise toward $100–$150 as you add email marketing, reviews and subscriptions ([ShopThemeDetector][std-apps]). The higher agency figures describe larger stores.
- **Typical first apps:** reviews, email marketing and a chat inbox. Judge.me, Klaviyo and Shopify Inbox are the three most installed apps ([Branvas][branvas], citing Store Leads data from April 24, 2026).
- **Unused apps:** among stores spending more than $100 a month, fewer than 60% of installed apps are actually in use ([Eightx][eightx]). A paid theme with built-in features can replace several apps.

## What setup costs, by who builds it

Professional setup runs $2,000–$10,000 with a freelancer and $8,000–$25,000 with a small agency ([Ecosire][ecosire]). Both main sources here sell Shopify services, so treat their ranges as the upper half of the market.

| Who builds it | Setup cost | Hourly rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You | $500–$3,000 in the first year, including theme, apps and photos ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | None |
| Marketplace freelancer, basic setup | $500–$2,000 ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) | $15–$100 on Upwork and Fiverr; $75–$150 on Storetasker ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) |
| Freelancer, full build | $2,000–$10,000 ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | $50–$120 in Western markets ([Ecosire][ecosire]) |
| Small agency | $8,000–$25,000 ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | $90–$250 ([Ecosire][ecosire]) |
| Established agency or Shopify Plus partner | $25,000–$50,000+ ([Ecosire][ecosire]); $25,000–$75,000+ with custom checkout work ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) | Senior US developers $150–$250+ ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) |

Junior Shopify developers in the US and Canada charge $50–$75 an hour, and offshore developers in Asia $15–$80 ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]). For how a store compares with other kinds of business websites, see [What does a small business website really cost in 2026?](/guides/small-business-website-cost)

## What a first year costs: three worked budgets

Three example stores show how the total splits. The inputs are assumptions picked from the ranges above, not survey results. Plan prices and card rates are Shopify's ([Shopify][shopify]), all three pay for their plan yearly, and email is $7 per person a month.

- **Store A:** you build it yourself on a free theme, on Basic, and sell $30,000 in the year (500 orders at $60).
- **Store B:** a freelancer builds it for $5,000 on a median-priced paid theme, on Basic, and it sells $150,000 (2,000 orders at $75).
- **Store C:** an agency builds it for $15,000, on Grow, and it sells $600,000 (7,500 orders at $80).

| Cost | Store A | Store B | Store C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | $0 | $5,000 | $15,000 |
| Theme | $0 (free) | $315 | Included in the build |
| Plan, billed yearly | $348 (Basic) | $348 (Basic) | $948 (Grow) |
| Apps | $240 ($20 a month) | $624 ($52 a month) | $3,600 ($300 a month) |
| Domain | $15 | $15 | $15 |
| Email | $84 (1 person) | $168 (2 people) | $252 (3 people) |
| **First year before card fees** | **$687** | **$6,470** | **$19,815** |
| Card fees | $1,020 | $4,950 | $18,450 |
| **First year in total** | **$1,707** | **$11,420** | **$38,265** |
| Year two, before card fees | $687 | $1,155 | $4,815 |

- **The card fee arithmetic:** store B pays 2.9% of $150,000 ($4,350) plus 30¢ on 2,000 orders ($600). Store C pays 2.7% of $600,000 ($16,200) plus 30¢ on 7,500 orders ($2,250).
- **What's left out:** product photos, advertising, shipping, Shopify Tax above $100,000 in sales, Maryland tax, and any maintenance retainer. A store like C would often add a retainer of $1,000–$3,000 a month ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]).
- **How to read it:** in every case, card fees are larger than all of Shopify's own charges combined. Setup fades after the first year: by year two, store B's fixed costs are about $96 a month, against about $413 a month in card fees.

## What makes a store cost more or less

Design, catalog size, integrations and migration drive the setup price. Your sales volume and your choice of payment processor drive the running cost.

| What drives the cost | Costs more | Costs less |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design | A fully custom theme at $10,000–$40,000+ ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | A free or paid theme with light changes |
| Catalog | Hundreds of products, each needing photos at $25–$100 and descriptions at $25–$150 ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | Launching with your best sellers, imported from a clean spreadsheet |
| Integrations | Connecting accounting, inventory or an ERP (enterprise resource planning) system; integration-heavy builds take 3–4 months ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | Ready-made connectors from the Shopify App Store before custom code |
| Migration | A large catalog, with old web addresses preserved for search, at $1,500–$15,000 ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | A new store with no history to move |
| Payments | Another card processor, which adds 0.6–2% of sales on Basic, Grow and Advanced ([Shopify][shopify]) | Shopify Payments |
| Apps | An app list nobody reviews | A paid theme with built-in features, and a yearly app review |
| Billing | Paying month to month | Paying yearly, about 25% less ([Shopify][shopify]) |
| Who builds it | Senior US developers at $150–$250+ an hour ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) | Marketplace freelancers at $15–$100 an hour, with more risk on quality ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) |

Ecosire also names two costs that never show up on an invoice. A heavily customized theme may no longer take its developer's updates cleanly. And a store that converts poorly, turning too few visitors into buyers, can lose more in sales than an agency would have charged ([Ecosire][ecosire]).

## How long it takes

A freelancer launches a small store in 2–4 weeks, and an agency build with custom design takes 6–12 weeks ([Ecosire][ecosire]).

| Route | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| You, with under 50 products | 3–6 weeks ([Elementor][elementor]) |
| Freelancer build | 2–4 weeks ([Ecosire][ecosire]) |
| Agency build with custom design and migration | 6–12 weeks ([Ecosire][ecosire]) |
| A project with many integrations | 3–4 months ([Ecosire][ecosire]) |
| Leaving Shopify for a custom platform | 6–12 months, with 30–90 days of running both side by side ([Space-O][spaceo]) |

Missing product content is the usual cause of delay. Elementor names unprepared content as the top reason website projects stall ([Elementor][elementor]). Have your product photos, descriptions, prices and shipping rules ready before work starts.

## When Shopify is the right platform, and when it isn't

For a store with under about $2 million a year in sales, Shopify almost always costs less than a custom build ([Space-O][spaceo]). A custom store costs $50,000–$300,000 or more to build and $3,500–$20,000 a month to run ([Space-O][spaceo]), against a Shopify plan of $29–$399 a month ([Shopify][shopify]).

| Cost | Shopify | Custom-built store |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build | $0–$25,000 for most small stores ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | $50,000–$80,000 for a basic catalog with checkout; $150,000–$300,000+ for a marketplace ([Space-O][spaceo]) |
| Hosting and servers | Included in the plan | $500–$5,000 a month ([Space-O][spaceo]) |
| Engineering upkeep | Handled by Shopify; a retainer is optional | $3,000–$15,000 a month for security, updates and fixes ([Space-O][spaceo]) |
| Time to launch | 2–12 weeks ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | 6–12 months ([Space-O][spaceo]) |

The custom figures come from a firm that builds custom software, so they're unlikely to be overstated.

**Shopify fits when:**

- You sell a normal catalog through a normal cart and checkout.
- Your sales are under $2 million a year, where custom development rarely pays off ([Space-O][spaceo]).
- Nobody on your staff can look after servers, security and payment compliance.
- You also sell in person and want one system for both.
- Your frustration is really with the theme or one app. A custom theme at $5,000–$15,000, or a different app, usually fixes it ([Space-O][spaceo]).

**Custom, or another platform, starts to make sense when** ([Space-O][spaceo]):

- Your business model doesn't fit a standard store: a marketplace with many sellers, complex subscription billing, or custom quoting for business buyers.
- You need integrations Shopify can't support, such as a proprietary inventory or compliance system.
- Your catalog is very large, with 100,000 or more products, complex filtering or real-time pricing.
- The fees are large in dollars. For a $5 million store that can't use Shopify Payments, Shopify's other-processor fees come to an estimated $25,000–$100,000 a year ([Space-O][spaceo]).

### WooCommerce, the usual middle path

WooCommerce, which you host yourself, is the usual alternative, and the cost claims conflict. One comparison says WooCommerce costs far less once a store passes about $50,000 a month in sales and would otherwise pay Shopify's other-processor fee ([Ecommerce CEO][ecommerceceo]). A Shopify agency says Shopify's all-in cost comes in 20–40% below a self-managed WooCommerce store for sellers under $5 million, once developer time, security patching and hosting are counted ([Presta][presta]). Both can be true: WooCommerce charges no platform fee, but you take on the hosting and upkeep.

## Red flags in a Shopify quote

Most Shopify overspending comes from four places: quotes that leave out card fees, app lists nobody reviews, custom work where a theme or app would do, and losing control of the store account. For red flags that apply to any web project, such as contracts and payment schedules, see the [small business website cost guide](/guides/small-business-website-cost).

| Red flag | Why it matters | What to look for instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A quote that never mentions card fees | Processing is about 3% of sales, and past about $10,000 a month it usually outweighs every other cost combined ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | A first-year budget that includes the plan, apps and card fees at your expected sales |
| A card processor other than Shopify Payments, with no reason given | It adds 0.6–2% to every sale on Basic, Grow and Advanced ([Shopify][shopify]) | A written reason, such as your products not being eligible for Shopify Payments |
| A dozen paid apps installed at launch | 98% of stores spend under $100 a month on apps ([Eightx][eightx]) | A short list showing each app's monthly price and what it replaces |
| A custom theme for a brand-new store | $10,000–$40,000+ before the store has shown it can sell ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | A paid theme now, and custom work once sales justify it |
| Heavy edits to the theme's code | The theme may stop taking its developer's updates cleanly ([Ecosire][ecosire]) | Changes made through the theme's own settings and sections where possible |
| The developer opens the Shopify account in their own name | You could lose the store, its domain and its payouts in a dispute | You open the account and invite the developer in as a collaborator |
| A theme sold outside the Theme Store at a steep discount | A Theme Store license covers one store and is final sale ([eSEOspace][eseospace-themes]), and a copied theme gets no updates or support | A theme bought in your own account |
| An upgrade to Advanced or Plus sold on the lower card rate alone | Advanced only pays for itself above roughly $110,000–$147,000 a month in card sales ([see above](#when-a-higher-plan-pays-for-itself)) | An upgrade tied to features you need |
| A redesign with no end date | One source describes a redesign that dragged on for six months ([Clean Commit][cleancommit]) | A fixed scope, milestones and a launch date in writing |
| Advice to leave Shopify for a custom build while sales are under $2 million a year | Custom development rarely pays off at that size ([Space-O][spaceo]) | A named limitation that Shopify, a theme or an app can't solve |

## If your business is in Maryland

A Maryland store deals with two different taxes: the 6% sales tax you collect from shoppers, and the 3% tax you pay on setup work and some business software. This section is general information, not tax advice. Check your situation with a tax professional, and confirm current rates with the Comptroller of Maryland, because the rules change.

### The sales tax your store collects

- **The rate:** a flat 6%, with no local add-ons. A business located in Maryland collects it on taxable sales to Maryland customers from the first sale ([Numeral][numeral]).
- **Sellers based outside Maryland** must collect once they pass $100,000 in sales or 200 separate transactions into the state in the current or previous calendar year ([Comptroller of Maryland][md-remote]). If you ship to customers in other states, ask a tax professional where you need to collect.
- **Marketplaces:** on Amazon, Etsy and similar marketplaces, the marketplace collects and pays the tax. If all your sales go through a marketplace, you don't have to register. If you also sell through your own Shopify store, you do ([Comptroller of Maryland][md-remote]).
- **The license:** a Maryland sales and use tax license is free, through Maryland Tax Connect ([Numeral][numeral-reg]).
- **Working out the tax:** Shopify Tax calculates the rates, and it's free until your store reaches $100,000 in sales ([Shopify Help][shopify-tax]).

### The 3% tax you pay on setup and software

- **Setup work:** since July 1, 2025, Maryland taxes custom web design, custom programming, software installation and consulting, and web hosting at 3% ([Comptroller of Maryland, Technical Bulletin 56][md-tax]). If you hire someone to build or customize your store, expect 3% on the invoice: $150 on a $5,000 build.
- **Software subscriptions:** the same bulletin taxes software sold as a subscription (often called software as a service, or SaaS) at 6% when it's bought for individual use and 3% when it's bought for use in a business's enterprise computer system ([Technical Bulletin 56][md-tax]). Expect Maryland tax on your Shopify plan and app bills, and ask your accountant which rate applies.

For more on how the 3% tax works, see [Maryland's 3% tax on IT and software services](/guides/maryland-tax-on-it-services).
